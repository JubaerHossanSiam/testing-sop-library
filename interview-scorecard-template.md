## Evaluation Scale

Use a simple evidence scale unless a role-specific approved scorecard defines another scale:

- **Strong evidence** — clearly demonstrates the required capability.
- **Adequate evidence** — demonstrates enough capability for the expected level.
- **Mixed / insufficient evidence** — evidence is incomplete, inconsistent, or requires further assessment.
- **Concern** — evidence indicates a meaningful mismatch or risk.
- **Not assessed** — this interview did not evaluate the criterion.
