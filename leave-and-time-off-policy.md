# Leave & Time-Off Policy

## Purpose

Define Blinto's leave entitlements and the rules for how leave is counted, requested, approved, carried forward, and handled when an exception is needed.

For the operational steps to request leave, notify stakeholders, and hand over work, see [Leave Request & Handover](./leave-request-and-handover.md).

## Leave at a Glance

| Leave type | Entitlement | Paid? | Carry forward? | Normal treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Annual Leave (EL / Earned Leave)** | **14 working days per calendar year as Blinto's planning baseline*** | Yes | Statutory eligible balance: yes | Planned leave; 1–5 working days can auto-approve when policy conditions are met |
| **Casual Leave (CL)** | **10 working days per calendar year** | Yes | No | Short personal needs; maximum 3 consecutive working days under CL |
| **Sick Leave (SL)** | **14 days per calendar year** | Yes | No | Notification-based for illness or medical need |
| **Maternity Leave** | **Up to 120 calendar days where statutory/contractual eligibility applies** | Yes where applicable | Not applicable | Special statutory leave |
| **Paternity Leave** | **7 working days per qualifying event** | Yes | No | Company benefit |
| **Bereavement Leave** | **3 working days per qualifying event** | Yes | No | Immediate-family bereavement |
| **Wedding Leave** | **3 working days for the employee's own wedding** | Yes | No | Planned special leave |
| **Public Holidays** | Per Blinto's official annual holiday calendar | Yes | Not applicable | Does not consume CL, SL, or Annual Leave balances |
| **Unpaid Leave** | As approved | No | Not applicable | Manual exception only |

> **Annual Leave note:** Blinto presents **14 working days** as the simple annual planning baseline for eligible employees. Where applicable Bangladesh law, an employment contract, or another approved benefit gives an employee a stronger earned-leave entitlement, the stronger entitlement applies. The statutory earned-leave calculation may therefore produce a balance higher than 14 days for an eligible employee.

## How Leave Days Are Counted

Blinto uses **working-day counting** for Annual Leave, Casual Leave, Paternity Leave, Bereavement Leave, and Wedding Leave unless a specific entitlement is legally defined in calendar days.

This means:

- only the employee's scheduled working days are deducted from the relevant working-day leave balance;
- weekly holidays/weekends inside the leave period are not deducted as extra Annual or Casual Leave days;
- company-declared public holidays inside a planned leave period do not consume Annual or Casual Leave;
- Blinto does **not** use a sandwich-leave rule to convert intervening weekly holidays or public holidays into paid-leave deductions;
- maternity leave and any other entitlement expressly defined in calendar days follow their specific calendar-day rule.

Example: if an employee is away for **9 calendar days** but only **5 of those days are scheduled working days**, a working-day leave category deducts **5 leave days**, not 9.

## Calendar Year & Employee Leave View

The leave year runs from **January 1 to December 31** unless a stronger statutory, contractual, or approved benefit requires different treatment.

For readability, the employee-facing HRMS should show the information employees need first:

| Annual Leave | Example display |
| --- | ---: |
| Annual planning entitlement | 14 days |
| Carried-forward eligible balance | 2 days |
| Used / booked | 5 days |
| Available for planning | 11 days |

The HRMS may keep technical accrual, statutory, and reconciliation calculations in the background or in an expanded detail view. Employees should not need to calculate `1 day per 18 qualifying days worked` themselves merely to understand their usable leave balance.

New joiners, first-year employees, partial-year employees, and employees whose legal/contractual eligibility differs from the standard configuration should see their **actual applicable HRMS balance**, not an assumed full-year balance.

## Core Principle

Leave within an employee's entitlement is a right, not a discretionary favor from management.

When an eligible planned-leave request meets the required balance, notice, duration, and other policy conditions, the HRMS should approve it automatically. Team Lead, People Operations/HR, and CEO receive visibility; they are not routine approvers of qualifying planned leave.

Where normal conditions are not met, the request moves to the manual exception workflow rather than being represented as automatically approved.

No Blinto policy may reduce a stronger entitlement under applicable Bangladesh law, an employment contract, or another formally approved company benefit.

## Leave Types & Detailed Rules

### Annual Leave (EL / Earned Leave)

For employee-facing planning, Blinto uses **14 working days per calendar year** as the normal Annual Leave baseline for an eligible regular employee.

Annual Leave is intended for planned rest, vacation, travel, and longer personal time away.

Where the Bangladesh statutory earned-leave rule applies, an eligible adult employee who has completed the applicable continuous-service requirement earns paid leave at **1 day for every 18 qualifying days worked**. That statutory calculation, contractual terms, or another approved benefit may result in an entitlement greater than Blinto's 14-day planning baseline. In that case, the **higher entitlement controls**.

The 14-day planning baseline therefore simplifies the employee experience; it does not operate as a legal ceiling that can reduce a stronger earned balance.

### Advance Planning Against Annual Leave

An eligible employee does not need to wait until every planned Annual Leave day has technically accrued before scheduling meaningful time off.

Where the employee is reasonably expected to remain eligible through the calendar year, the HRMS may allow planned use against the employee's projected annual entitlement, subject to the normal balance, notice, duration, and business-continuity rules.

The employee-facing view should remain simple. Where needed, People Operations may maintain a more detailed ledger containing:

- statutory/accrued EL to date;
- projected Annual Leave entitlement;
- carried-forward EL;
- Annual Leave already used/booked;
- any advance Annual Leave reconciliation requirement.

If employment ends or eligibility materially changes before an advance balance has been fully earned, People Operations must reconcile the balance only to the extent permitted by applicable law, employment terms, and valid company process. This policy does not authorize an unlawful salary deduction or forfeiture of a stronger entitlement.

### Annual Leave Carry-Forward

Unused statutory earned leave must be carried forward and accumulated where applicable Bangladesh law requires it.

The HRMS should:

- preserve eligible carried-forward balances;
- consume the **oldest eligible Annual Leave first**;
- apply the statutory accumulation ceiling applicable to the employee/establishment;
- preserve any stronger contractual or approved company benefit.

People Operations and Team Leads should proactively support employees in taking accumulated leave rather than allowing avoidable balances to build indefinitely.

### Casual Leave (CL)

- **10 paid working days per calendar year.**
- Intended for personal matters, short breaks, and ordinary short-term personal needs.
- May be taken as a half day where applicable.
- Maximum **3 consecutive working days** under the Casual Leave category.
- Does not carry forward into the next calendar year.

### Sick Leave (SL)

- **14 paid days per calendar year.**
- Intended for illness, medical need, or health emergency.
- Does not carry forward into the next calendar year.
- Sick Leave is notification-based; an employee should not wait for ordinary advance approval before responding to a genuine health need.
- Blinto normally does not require medical evidence for **1–2 consecutive sick days**.
- For **3 or more consecutive sick days**, People Operations may require reasonable medical evidence/documentation. Where applicable law requires medical certification, People Operations may request the documentation needed for compliance.

### Maternity Leave

Eligible employees receive maternity leave according to applicable Bangladesh statutory entitlement and eligibility rules. Blinto's current baseline is **up to 120 paid calendar days where legally/contractually applicable**.

Where timing is reasonably foreseeable, the employee should provide the required information/documentation at least **30 calendar days before** the expected leave start. Genuine medical or childbirth-related timing changes do not remove the underlying entitlement.

### Paternity Leave

Blinto provides **7 paid working days** of paternity leave as a company benefit for eligible employees.

Where reasonably foreseeable, submit at least **7 calendar days before** the expected start. Unforeseen childbirth timing or emergency circumstances should be handled through notification rather than penalized for insufficient notice.

### Bereavement Leave

Blinto provides **3 paid working days** for a qualifying immediate-family bereavement. The employee should notify the Team Lead/People Operations as soon as reasonably possible.

### Wedding Leave

Blinto provides **3 paid working days** for the employee's own wedding. Submit at least **21 calendar days before** the leave start for auto-approval.

### Public Holidays

Public holidays are governed by Blinto's official annual holiday calendar and do not consume CL, SL, or Annual Leave balances except where applicable law specifically requires different treatment for a particular leave period.

### Unpaid Leave

Unpaid Leave is an exception rather than an automatic entitlement under this company policy. It requires manual review/approval, including when an employee requests time away beyond an available paid-leave balance.

## Planned Leave Duration Rules

Blinto handles longer planned absence through stronger planning and approval requirements, **not** by counting weekends or public holidays as additional Annual Leave.

| Requested planned leave | Normal treatment |
| --- | --- |
| **1–5 working days** | Auto-approved when eligibility, balance, notice, and other policy conditions are met |
| **6–10 working days** | Manual review: Team Lead → People Operations/HR |
| **11+ working days** | Extended-leave review with a documented continuity/handover plan; may require People Operations and CEO approval depending on materiality, unpaid leave, or exception needed |

An extended absence may combine available paid leave with approved Unpaid Leave or another applicable entitlement. Approval of an extended absence does not increase the employee's paid-leave balance.

| Leave type | Auto-approved when | Auto-approved duration | Longer / exceptional request |

## HRMS Outcomes

### 1. Auto-Approved

Use when all applicable eligibility, balance, notice, duration, and policy conditions are met.

The system should immediately record the leave and notify the employee, Team Lead, People Operations/HR, and CEO.

### 2. Manual Approval Required

Use when the requested leave is potentially valid but falls outside an auto-approval boundary, for example:

- insufficient advance notice;
- Annual Leave request longer than 5 working days;
- an 11+ working-day extended absence;
- projected Annual Leave eligibility is uncertain;
- exceptional extension of a special leave;
- unusual eligibility/documentation issue;
- Unpaid Leave or another policy exception.

Manual review does not mean the employee has done something wrong. It means delivery, eligibility, or exception handling requires human alignment.

### 3. Not Requestable Under This Leave Type

Use when the selected category has a hard boundary, for example:

- Casual Leave longer than 3 consecutive working days;
- requested paid leave exceeds the employee's available/eligible balance and no applicable entitlement supports it;
- the employee selected an inapplicable leave category.

Where possible, the HRMS should suggest a valid alternative, such as Annual Leave, Unpaid Leave, or another applicable special-leave route.

## Manual Approval Ownership

For ordinary notice or duration exceptions:

**Team Lead → People Operations/HR**

The Team Lead assesses delivery/coverage implications. People Operations validates entitlement, records, and consistent policy treatment.

CEO approval is reserved for material exceptions, significant unpaid/extended leave, or another case requiring a company-level decision. CEO visibility does not make the CEO a routine leave approver.

## Auto-Approved Leave & Business Conflicts

A Team Lead cannot unilaterally cancel an auto-approved leave simply because it later becomes inconvenient.

If a genuine material delivery conflict exists, the Team Lead should discuss it directly with the employee and attempt to mutually agree on an alternative. If agreement cannot be reached, escalate to People Operations/HR for resolution consistent with employee entitlement and applicable law.

## Leave Planning & Handover

Employees should use planned leave responsibly and provide the required notice whenever the need is foreseeable.

Team Leads are responsible for capacity planning, backup coverage, and reducing single-person dependency so legitimate leave does not become a recurring delivery crisis.

For extended leave, the handover should be proportionate to the length and operational impact of the absence. Follow [Leave Request & Handover](./leave-request-and-handover.md).

## Probationary Employees, New Joiners & Interns

Probationary status does not by itself reduce a statutory leave entitlement that applies to an employee.

People Operations must apply CL, SL, Annual/EL eligibility, accrual, and any proportional treatment according to applicable Bangladesh law, the individual's employment status and contract, and any stronger approved Blinto benefit.

Intern leave treatment must similarly follow the applicable legal classification, internship terms, and approved Blinto benefit. The HRMS must not impose a blanket rule that reduces a statutory or contractual entitlement.
