# Employee Offboarding

## Purpose

Provide a controlled process for ending an employee/intern engagement while protecting company/client access, preserving required knowledge and records, recovering assets, and closing the relationship professionally.

Use the [Employee Offboarding Checklist](./employee-offboarding-checklist.md) to execute and verify each departure. This SOP owns the process and approval rules.

This SOP covers operational offboarding. Statutory rights, notice, severance, and other legal/contractual terms must follow the applicable contract, company policy, and law.

## When to Use

Use for resignation, termination, end of internship, end of fixed-term employment where applicable, or another departure requiring company access/records/assets to be closed.

## Owner

People Operations coordinates offboarding. The Team/Department Lead owns work handover. Responsible system/account owners support access removal.

## Termination Approval

For involuntary termination, the standard approval flow is:

**Team/Department Lead recommendation → People Operations/HR review → CEO final approval**

The Team/Department Lead documents the basis for the recommendation. People Operations/HR reviews the case, records, applicable process, and employment terms. The CEO provides final authorization before termination is executed, except where immediate protective action is required for a serious security/safety risk and management must first restrict access or protect company/client assets.

## Procedure

### 1. Confirm the departure

People Operations confirms the departure basis, final/effective working date, applicable notice/contract instructions, responsible manager/lead, confidentiality requirements, and urgency of access removal.

### 2. Create the offboarding plan

Identify actions and owners for work/client handover, system/account access, credentials, company assets, employment/HR records, final administrative/financial processing, and required communication.

### 3. Complete work and knowledge handover

Identify open work, commitments, client/vendor context, blockers/risks, files/documentation, and reusable process knowledge not yet captured in the SOP Library. Missing reusable knowledge should be proposed to the canonical library rather than stored as an exit-only duplicate.

### 4. Transfer ownership

Reassign applicable tasks/projects, documents/files, client/project ownership, recurring responsibilities, calendars/meetings, and shared/system ownership. Verify transfer.

### 5. Remove/revoke access

At the appropriate effective time, remove applicable company/client access including identity/email, ClickUp, Google Workspace/Drive, communication tools, password-manager vaults, GitHub, hosting/cloud/DNS, client systems, and privileged sessions/tokens.

Where individual revocation is insufficient because a shared credential was exposed, change or invalidate that credential. Do not perform unnecessary blanket password rotation where individual access can be safely revoked.

### 6. Recover company assets and information

Recover applicable company property and confirm required company/client files/work products are stored in approved company-controlled locations.

### 7. Complete exit administration

People Operations completes required records and coordinates final administrative/financial actions according to the governing contract, policy, and applicable law. Where appropriate, collect exit feedback and issue approved completion/employment documents.

### 8. Communicate the transition

Notify relevant stakeholders only as necessary for continuity and security. Keep communication factual and avoid confidential employment details.

### 9. Verify closeout

Complete and retain the applicable [Employee Offboarding Checklist](./employee-offboarding-checklist.md). Offboarding is complete when ownership is transferred, required access is removed, assets are accounted for, administration is complete or assigned, stakeholders know the new contact/owner, and unresolved security/client/financial/legal items have an owner.

## Immediate / High-Risk Departure

For a high-risk or immediate departure, prioritize coordinated access restriction/revocation, evidence preservation, company/client asset protection, and controlled communication. Protective security action may occur immediately when necessary, while the formal employment decision follows required approval and applicable legal/contractual process.

## Exceptions / Escalation

Escalate when a contractual/legal requirement is uncertain, company/client data appears missing, privileged access remains, company assets are not returned, misconduct/security compromise/fraud/data loss is suspected, or client continuity is at risk.
